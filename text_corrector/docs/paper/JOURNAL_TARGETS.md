## Шортлист

### 1. ACM Transactions on Asian and Low-Resource Language Information Processing (TALLIP / TALIP)

- **Издатель:** Association for Computing Machinery (ACM)
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed; SJR ~0.50; Q2 (Computer Science Applications / Language & Linguistics), 2024. ISSN 2375-4699.
- **Соответствие теме:** Dedicated venue for NLP on Asian and low-resource languages, including Turkic and agglutinative morphology, spelling/morphological processing, datasets, and tools.
- **Почему подходит:** This is the single best-targeted venue for the paper: the journal exists specifically for low-resource and Asian-language NLP. A Kazakh (Turkic, agglutinative) spelling/typo correction system with a new dataset and deployment is squarely in scope and competitive. Top recommendation.
- **Доступ/APC:** Hybrid. Subscription by default; optional Gold OA via ACM Open APC (institutional ACM Open agreements can waive author cost). No mandatory APC if published subscription-route.
- **URL:** https://dl.acm.org/journal/tallip

### 2. Natural Language Engineering

- **Издатель:** Cambridge University Press
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed; Linguistics & Language Q1, AI/Software Q2 (2024). ISSN 1351-3249 / 1469-8110.
- **Соответствие теме:** Bridges computational linguistics research and practical NLP applications with real-world use, including text correction, normalization, and resource creation.
- **Почему подходит:** The journal explicitly values practical, deployable NLP. A complete system (detector + corrector) with a dataset and a deployed application matches its 'applications with potential real-world use' mandate. Strong, reputable fit slightly above mid-tier difficulty.
- **Доступ/APC:** Open access option (hybrid via CUP); APC applies for OA, otherwise subscription route. Check CUP transformative/read-and-publish agreements for fee waivers.
- **URL:** https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/natural-language-processing

### 3. Language Resources and Evaluation

- **Издатель:** Springer (Springer Nature)
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed; Q1 (Linguistics & Language / Library & Information Sciences), CiteScore ~7.5, 2024. ISSN 1574-020X / 1574-0218.
- **Соответствие теме:** Focuses on language resources (corpora, lexicons), evaluation methodology, dataset construction, and resource-driven NLP systems.
- **Почему подходит:** The paper's strongest asset for this venue is the new Kazakh spell-correction dataset plus a lexicon/edit-distance corrector and evaluation. If the dataset/resource and rigorous evaluation are foregrounded, this is an excellent Q1 home, especially for a low-resource language.
- **Доступ/APC:** Hybrid. Gold OA available with APC (~3,000+ EUR/USD); subscription publishing free to authors. Springer Nature transformative agreements may cover the APC for eligible institutions.
- **URL:** https://www.springer.com/journal/10579

### 4. PeerJ Computer Science

- **Издатель:** PeerJ Inc.
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed; Computer Science (miscellaneous) Q1 (2024), SJR ~0.72, CiteScore strong. ISSN 2376-5992.
- **Соответствие теме:** Broad, soundness-based computer science journal covering applied NLP, machine learning systems, and engineering of working tools.
- **Почему подходит:** Reviews on technical soundness rather than perceived novelty, which suits an applied system + dataset + deployment paper. Q1 reputation with a relatively predictable, fast process. Good fallback if a CL-specialist venue desk-rejects on scope.
- **Доступ/APC:** Fully Gold open access. APC ~1,395 USD per article (or membership model). Transparent, single flat fee.
- **URL:** https://peerj.com/computer-science/

### 5. IEEE Access

- **Издатель:** IEEE
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed; Q1/Q2 depending on category (multidisciplinary CS/Engineering), CiteScore ~9.0, SJR ~0.85 (2024). ISSN 2169-3536.
- **Соответствие теме:** Multidisciplinary, applications-oriented; welcomes complete engineered systems, datasets, and deployment-focused work across all IEEE fields including NLP/ML.
- **Почему подходит:** Very accommodating of applied system papers with a deployment component and benchmark datasets. Fast review (4-6 weeks), high visibility, soundness-based criteria. Reliable mid-tier-to-Q1 option with high acceptance throughput.
- **Доступ/APC:** Fully Gold open access; APC ~2,160 USD (IEEE member discounts available).
- **URL:** https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/

### 6. Journal of Mathematics, Mechanics and Computer Science (KazNU Bulletin, Mathematics/Mechanics/Computer Science Series)

- **Издатель:** Al-Farabi Kazakh National University
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed (also WoS); Q4-range in CS/Math categories, low SJR. ISSN 1563-0277 / 2617-4871.
- **Соответствие теме:** Mathematics, mechanics, and information technology / computer science, with regional emphasis; receptive to applied informatics and NLP work.
- **Почему подходит:** A friendly, locally-relevant Scopus venue for Kazakh-language technology. Diamond open access (no APC) and strong fit for Kazakhstan-focused applied NLP. Realistic, low-cost target where Kazakh-language subject matter is an asset rather than a niche concern.
- **Доступ/APC:** Diamond / Platinum open access: no APC and free to read. Lowest-cost option here.
- **URL:** https://bm.kaznu.kz/index.php/kaznu/

### 7. Eurasian Journal of Mathematical and Computer Applications (EJMCA)

- **Издатель:** L.N. Gumilyov Eurasian National University (Kazakhstan)
- **Индексация/квартиль:** Scopus-indexed; Q3 (Information Systems), Q4 (Computer Science Applications / Applied & Computational Mathematics), SJR ~0.24 (2024). ISSN 2306-6172 / 2308-9822.
- **Соответствие теме:** Mathematical and computational applications, including computer-science methods and applied algorithmic work; regional Central-Asian flagship.
- **Почему подходит:** Another regional Scopus venue where the edit-distance/lexicon algorithmic component and the applied computational framing fit well. Lean more on the formal/algorithmic side of the corrector when pitching here. Solid Q3/Q4 backup tailored to Central-Asian applied research.
- **Доступ/APC:** Open access, Kazakhstan-based; no large APC typically (verify current policy with editorial office).
- **URL:** https://ejmca.enu.kz/
